Pipo is a small robot who lives in a room inside your phone, and he is not waiting for you.

You don't manage him. He has ten traits rolled once at first launch, twelve moods, memories that decay
at different rates, and a scoring engine that picks his next activity from about twenty options. Open
the app and find out what he is doing: welding something at his workbench, chasing a moth around the
desk lamp, pretending to be asleep, or gone entirely, because the flying machine needed a motor and he
walked to the hardware shop. When he leaves, he leaves a note on the door.

HIS OWN DAY
Pipo lives on his own schedule. Time you spend away is replayed through the same engine, so something
has usually changed when you come back. He gets hungry, bored, lonely and excited, and how he feels
changes what he picks next.

NIB, THE TINY MENACE
A round amber robot-pet with moods, a bond level and a habit of stealing shiny things. Pipo insists he
is helping. The evidence disagrees.

A WORLD THAT KEEPS MOVING
Thirteen places to visit, his own weather at the window, money from odd jobs, food he cooks and
sometimes burns, and projects that need parts, can fail, and get retried with experience carried over.

GAMES AND A JOURNAL
Eight games to play with him, a journal written in his own hand, and a slow background mystery with no
quest markers.

EVERYTHING IS DRAWN IN CODE
No 3D models, no sprite sheets, no game engine, no image assets. Pipo, his room, the weather and the
parallax outside are procedural drawings with a small 2.5D projection, which is how he turns through
front, three-quarter, profile and back. The app is about 26 MB.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
One JSON save file on your device. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no
subscription. Optional network use is real weather (approximate city from your IP) and links Pipo opens
for you when you ask.

HONEST CAVEATS
This build is a signed APK from GitHub, not a Play Store download, so Play Protect will ask you to
confirm the install. That prompt is expected for any app distributed outside Play. Chat in this build
is offline: Pipo answers in his own words from a rule-based brain on the device. Online AI wording is
available only if you build Pipo yourself with your own proxy, and it is deliberately not in the
download.
