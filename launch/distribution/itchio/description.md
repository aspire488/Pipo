# Pipo

**A little robot who lives in a room on your Android phone and gets on with his own day.**

Pipo is not an assistant. There are no tasks, no streaks and no score. He has his own traits, moods and
memories, and he decides what to do next — you open the app and find out what happened while you were
away.

- **His own life:** ten traits, twelve moods and decaying memories, scored into 20+ autonomous
  activities. Sometimes he works at his bench, sometimes he lies on the floor, occasionally he walks to
  the hardware shop and leaves a note on the door.
- **Nib**, his small robot pet, with its own moods, a bond level and a habit of stealing shiny things.
- **A world that keeps moving:** thirteen places to visit, his own weather, money from odd jobs, food
  he cooks (and sometimes burns), and projects that need parts and can fail.
- **Eight games**, a journal written in his own hand, and a slow background mystery with no quest
  markers.
- **Drawn entirely in code** — no 3D models, no sprite sheets, no game engine. About 26 MB.

## Install

1. Download the APK (below).
2. Open it on your phone.
3. If prompted, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.
4. Tap **Install anyway** if Play Protect asks.

**This is a sideload from GitHub, not the Play Store.** Play Protect will warn you that the app is
unverified. That is expected for any app distributed outside Play, and it is a verification warning, not
a malware detection. **Do not disable Play Protect.**
